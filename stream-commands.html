Stream Commands.

Commands available to viewers and moderators across the Twitch tools built for the stream.

Chat TTS

Jerry's Chat Controls

Viewer commands for the chat-to-voice system, sound effects, and stream games.

General

!help !commands
Show the available viewer and moderator commands.
!sounds
List the enabled sound effects.
#<sound>
Play a named sound effect when sound effects are enabled.
?<key>
Send a configured keyboard input to the stream setup.
+death
Add one to the on-stream death counter.

Slots & Points

!slot !slots
Spin the chat slot machine. A per-viewer cooldown applies.
!scoreboard !leaderboard
Show the top five slot-machine scores.
!points !wallet !balance
Show your points, active perks, curses, insurance, bounties, and title.
!spend <item> [args]
Buy a perk from the point shop. Full options and costs are below.

Slots & Points

Point Shop

Spend points to improve your next spin, influence another viewer, or customise the leaderboard.

Spin Perks

!spend insurance 200 pts
Add one insurance charge. It absorbs the next six penalty.
!spend jackpot 500 pts
Boost your next spin with a guaranteed high-value seven or chest reel.
!spend double 100 pts
Double the points from your next winning spin. It is not spent on a loss.
!spend mystery 250 pts
Open a mystery box: it can award a jackpot, insurance, or a boost, but may also curse you or do nothing.

Target Another Viewer

!spend curse @viewer 300 pts
Make six appear on the target's next spin. Use random instead of a viewer to pick an active target.
!spend gift @viewer <amount>
Transfer the chosen number of your points to another viewer.
!spend steal @viewer 150 pts
Attempt to steal up to 300 points. It succeeds 25% of the time; on failure, the 150-point fee is lost.
!spend bounty @viewer <amount>
Place your points in a bounty pool on a target. The next jackpot winner claims the pool.

Leaderboard Cosmetics

!spend color <color> 500 pts
Set your leaderboard name colour. Available colours: gold, red, purple, cyan, green, orange, or pink.
!spend title <name> 1000 pts
Set a custom leaderboard title, up to 15 characters long.

How a Spin Pays

3 matching symbols
Jackpot: the symbol value is multiplied by three.
2 matching symbols
Mini-win: receive the matching symbol's value.
six
A bad result: lose 1000 points unless an insurance charge blocks it.
cherry / lemon / bell / clover / diamond / chest / seven
Values range from 10 to 150 points; chest and seven are the highest-value symbols.
