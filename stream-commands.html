Stream Commands.

Commands available to viewers and moderators across the Twitch tools built for the stream.

Chat TTS

Jerry's Chat Controls

Viewer commands for the chat-to-voice system, sound effects, and stream games.

General

!help !commands
Show the available viewer and moderator commands.
!sounds
List the enabled sound effects.
#<sound>
Play a named sound effect when sound effects are enabled.
?<key>
Send a configured keyboard input to the stream setup.
+death
Add one to the on-stream death counter.

Slots & Points

!slot !slots
Spin the chat slot machine. A per-viewer cooldown applies.
!scoreboard !leaderboard
Show the top five slot-machine scores.
!points !wallet !balance
Show your points, active perks, curses, insurance, bounties, and title.
!spend <item> [args]
Spend points on perks such as insurance, curse, jackpot, double, gift, steal, bounty, mystery, colour, or title.

Stream Game

Battle Arena [shelved]

An earlier real-time chat-controlled arena game, retained here as part of the stream toolkit.

Enter & Fight

!spawn
Spawn your fighter in the arena.
!attack [target]
Attack another fighter or the active boss.
!defend
Take a defensive combat action.
!target [name]
Set your target.
!boss [args]
Use boss-related actions when a boss encounter is active.
!heal
Restore health.
!status
Show your fighter's current status.

Explore & Recover

!classes [class]
View or select available character classes.
!left !right
Move horizontally through the arena.
!up !down
Move vertically through the arena.
!pickup
Pick up an item in the arena.
!flee
Attempt to escape combat.
!die
Trigger the death or respawn flow.

Just Chatting Game

Dollhouse

Every viewer becomes a coloured sprite in a shared interactive space on stream.

Viewer Actions

!flip
Flip your own sprite upside down for a moment.
!spin
Make your own sprite spin.
!stab <viewer>
Chase and stab another viewer's sprite when the knife game is active. Accepts names with or without @.
